📖 Description

Builds a printable gait report (HTML + PDF) from a Marcha Fácil / gait_auto analysis folder.

Page 1, for the patient and the therapist

  • Identification: participant, age, affected side, therapist, institution, recording.
  • A plain-language summary. Each parameter is compared with its reference range, with a short explanation when it is outside the range (e.g. "the foot stays on the floor longer (seeking stability)").
  • Temporal parameters: cadence, stance, swing, double support, stride time, left/right difference and peak knee flexion in swing. Values outside the reference range are shown in bold and colour.
  • Hip, knee and ankle angles over the gait cycle (mean ± SD per side).
  • Four frames of the MP Angles video: heel strike, mid-stance, toe-off and mid-swing.
  • The MP Angles skeleton-sequence image for every analysed camera (one image per camera, captioned "side view" or "frontal view (coronal)"): a grid of frames across the walk with the detected skeleton and joint angle values — built by mpangles.plot_stick_sequence_mpangles, already generated when the skeleton + angles video is rendered.

Page 2, technical (for the therapist)

  • Range of motion per joint and side, compared with references.
  • MP Angles statistics split into Right / Left / Neck tables instead of one 15-column table. Each table gives the mean, SD, minimum and maximum with the time they happen, range, between-cycle variability and the number of extreme points. The affected side is tagged.
  • Points of extreme oscillation, in bold red, with the time to check in the video:
  • off pattern: the angle departs from the mean of the person's other cycles, at the same % of the cycle, by more than 2.5 between-cycle SDs (and at least 10°). This needs at least 3 cycles of that side. The cycle under test is left out of its own reference, so an odd cycle cannot hide itself. A z-score over the whole walk is not used, because it flags the normal ~60° knee flexion of swing.
  • abrupt change: a frame-to-frame jump larger than max(15°, 6 × the median jump). This can be instability, compensation or a tracking error.
  • Gait cycles, method and references.

Screen-only appendix: the frame-by-frame angle tables, split by side, with the extreme values highlighted. This section is not printed.

The PDF is printed by a Chromium-based browser in headless mode (Chrome, Chromium or Edge). Without one, the HTML has a "Print / save PDF" button.

📐 Reference ranges

These are approximate ranges around the mean values of healthy adults walking at a self-selected speed (Perry & Burnfield 2010; symmetry index: Robinson et al. 1987):

ParameterRange
Cadence100–125 steps/min
Stance / swing57–63 % / 37–43 % of the cycle
Double support16–24 % of the cycle
Stride time0.95–1.25 s
Left/right difference (stance)≤ 10 %
Peak knee flexion in swing55–65°
Range of motion: hip / knee / ankle35–45° / 55–70° / 25–35°

Hip and ankle come from MP Angles' unsigned 2D angles (trunk–thigh, shank–foot), so their range of motion is an approximation.

💻 CLI

uv run python vaila/gait_report.py --analysis <gait_analysis_dir> \
    [--lang pt en] [--participant ID] [--age 54] [--side right|left|both] \
    [--therapist NAME] [--institution NAME] [--no-pdf]

⚠️ Limitations

  • The report is generated to support an assessment. It does not replace clinical assessment.
  • With fewer than 3 cycles per side, only abrupt changes are checked, and the report says so.
  • 2D angles in the camera plane, with no calibration.
